# Version 0.5 plan: neural networks (fixed before any network was trained)

Written on 2026-09-18. It applies the approved [evaluation protocol](evaluation_protocol.md) to neural
networks and does not change it. Settings are in `config.yaml` → `deep`.

## Goal

Build at least one neural network suited to one-dimensional spectra and compare it honestly with the
classical models of Versions 0.3 and 0.4. If the classical models are better, that is the result.

## Architectures (and why these)

| Model | Shape | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **MLP** | input → 256 → 64 → 1 (or 512 → 128), ReLU, dropout | the standard neural baseline for fixed-length vectors; the DRIAMS authors' code also includes an MLP |
| **1-D CNN** | 2–3 blocks of (convolution → batch norm → ReLU → max-pool), then global average pooling → 1 | a spectrum is ordered along m/z, so neighbouring bins belong to the same peak. Convolutions share weights along that axis and need far fewer parameters (about 5,000 against 1.5 million for the MLP), which suits 2,977 training samples |

**Not used:** Temporal Convolutional Networks, Transformers and other large architectures. With 2,977
training spectra they would add capacity and tuning choices without a reason to expect a gain; the
project rules ask for the simplest model that is scientifically justified.

## Training

- **Loss:** binary cross-entropy on the resistance label. Class imbalance is handled by a positive class
  weight (the same idea as the class weights of Version 0.4), searched as a setting.
- **Optimiser:** AdamW, learning rate and weight decay searched.
- **Early stopping:** on an inner split of the **training part** (15 % of it), monitoring AUROC, patience
  8 epochs, at most 60 epochs. The best epoch's weights are restored (checkpointing in memory).
  - **Known simplification:** that inner split is stratified but not patient-grouped, because scikit-learn
    does not pass patient groups through the search to the model. A patient can appear on both sides of
    the inner split, which can make early stopping slightly optimistic. It cannot leak validation or test
    data, and the cross-validation score is unaffected.
- **Loss curves** of the final models are saved and plotted (training and inner-validation, per epoch).
- **Seeds:** networks are stochastic, so the final model is trained with seeds 42–46; seed 42 is reported
  and can be saved.

## Procedure (dataset `ecoli_ciprofloxacin`, `random` split)

Identical to Version 0.4, so the numbers are comparable:

1. Every setting is scored by 5-fold patient-grouped cross-validation **inside the training part**.
2. The setting with the highest mean AUROC wins per family.
3. The winner is refitted on the whole training part, with sigmoid calibration fitted on out-of-fold
   predictions of the same folds.
4. **Validation** picks the cut-off (highest threshold with sensitivity ≥ 0.90) and the best family.
5. **Test** (only with `--evaluate-test`, once per model, logged): the `random` test part for both
   families, plus the Version 0.4 model re-scored for a paired comparison.
   - The patient-overlap check is repeated **only if a network is chosen as the new saved model**;
     otherwise it would describe a model nobody uses. Versions 0.3 and 0.4 both found no effect.
   - The `temporal` and `external` test parts stay locked until Version 0.7.

## Search spaces

Sized from measured epoch times on this laptop (CPU only): MLP 0.38 s/epoch on 6,000 bins and 0.11 s on
1,000 coarser bins; CNN 3.7 s and 0.71 s.

| Family | Settings | Count |
|---|---|---|
| MLP | features: all 6,000 bins or 1,000 coarser 18 Da bins; hidden sizes (256, 64) or (512, 128); dropout 0.2 or 0.5; positive class weight on or off | 16 |
| 1-D CNN | features: 6,000 bins (2 settings: kernel 9 or 25, channels 16/32) or 1,000 coarser bins (4 settings: kernel 9 or 15, channels 16/32 or 32/64) | 6 |

Learning rate 1e-3 and weight decay 1e-4 are fixed for both; batch size 64. Inputs are standardised with
statistics from the training rows only, inside the pipeline.

**Budget:** roughly 1.5–2 hours on this laptop. Searches and fitted models are cached exactly as in
Version 0.4, so the test run reuses them.

## Reporting

- Every setting with its cross-validation result, the chosen setting per family, and loss curves.
- Test results with 95 % intervals, and the paired difference to the Version 0.4 model.
- The comparison table of all versions: accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, training and
  inference time.
- **If the networks do not beat the classical models, the report says so plainly.** With 2,977 training
  samples and 6,000 features, that is a likely outcome and a legitimate finding.

## Changes carried over

- The RBF SVM is dropped from Version 0.5 onwards: it scored lowest in Version 0.4, took 84 minutes to
  search and predicts about 400 times slower than LightGBM. Its Version 0.4 numbers stay in the tables.
- The small robustness fixes to `src/tuning.py` that Version 0.4 deferred are made now. They do not change
  results for the configured settings, but they change the file, so the Version 0.4 cache no longer
  matches. Version 0.4's results and saved model are already recorded; re-running it would recompute.

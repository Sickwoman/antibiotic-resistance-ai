MALDI-TOF resistance prediction: the frozen ciprofloxacin model

Research prototype. Synthetic spectra only. Not a diagnostic, not a substitute for antimicrobial susceptibility testing, and never a treatment recommendation.

What this project is

The project asks whether routine MALDI-TOF mass spectra, already recorded to identify bacteria, carry a signal about E. coli's resistance to ciprofloxacin. A LightGBM model was trained on 2,977 spectra from one Swiss hospital (DRIAMS-A) and then frozen: its preprocessing, weights and research threshold have not changed since. In Version 2.0 it was evaluated once, under a pre-registered protocol, on an independent hospital's public data (MARISMa, Madrid).

This page shows the pipeline at work on three synthetic spectra, scored by the real frozen model on this computer, beside the evaluation's published results. A synthetic spectrum comes from no organism, so its score demonstrates the pipeline and says nothing about any bacterium.
